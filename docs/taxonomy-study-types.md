# Taxonomy — Study Types (PEA / PFS / FS)

> Gate the target level **before** composing. The level determines confidence language,
> allowed use — and what the input corpus must contain. The weakest link sets the level.

| Level | Also called | Resource basis for economics | Cost accuracy* | Engineering | Valid use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PEA** | Scoping, Preliminary Economic Assessment | May include **Inferred** resources (must disclose) | ±35–50% | Conceptual, trade-offs open | Screening only; **not** a basis for reserves or production decision |
| **PFS** | Pre-Feasibility | Predominantly **Indicated + Measured**; Inferred excluded from economics in most regimes | ±20–25% | Preferred options selected, alternatives narrowed | Justifies advancement to FS; supports early permitting/financing discussions |
| **FS** | Feasibility, Definitive / Bankable FS | **Measured + Indicated → Proven + Probable reserves** only | ±10–15% | Detailed design, firm quotes, execution plan | Production decision, financing, reserve declaration |

\* Accuracy bands are industry rules of thumb (AACE Class 4/3/2 analogues), not guarantees.
Always report the study's **own stated accuracy** when given; otherwise label as indicative.

## Input maturity gates (minimum corpus to claim each level)

| Gate | PEA | PFS | FS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resources | Inferred allowed in economics (disclosed) | M+I drive economics; Inferred excluded | M+I converted to P&P reserves with modifying factors |
| Metallurgy | Bench-scale recoveries | Pilot testwork, narrowing flowsheet | Pilot/operational, locked flowsheet |
| Costs | Factored estimates | Partial quotes, AACE-3-ish build-up | Vendor quotes, execution plan, AACE-2-ish |
| Design | Conceptual options open | Preferred option selected | Detailed design + schedule |

Missing a gate → cap the study at the lower level and put the shortfall in the §26
work program with a budget.

## Classification signals

- Title page / QP certificates / Section 1 (Summary) state the level explicitly — trust that first.
- PEA red flags: "preliminary in nature," "includes Inferred resources," no reserve declaration.
- PFS → FS graduation: reserve statement appears, geotech/hydro/met testwork matures,
  CAPEX moves from factored estimates to quoted packages.
- Canadian reports cite **NI 43-101**; Australian reports cite **JORC Code** — record which regime applies.

## Confidence language (use verbatim tone)

- PEA: "conceptual," "preliminary," "not a production decision."
- PFS: "demonstrates technical and economic viability subject to FS confirmation."
- FS: "supports a production decision / financing basis at stated assumptions."
